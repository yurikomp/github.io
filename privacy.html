Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

rclone-inbox is a private configuration of rclone used by a single person (the owner) with the owner's own Google account.

What data is accessed

Through the Google Drive API, rclone-inbox can list, read, create, update and delete files in the owner's Google Drive. It is used only to upload and manage the owner's own notes in the owner's own Drive.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only for the purpose above. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or analytics. Data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own devices.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings, under third-party apps with account access.
